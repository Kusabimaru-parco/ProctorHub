📖 How to get your keys (100% Free)
Step 1: Get your Auth Token
  1. Create a free account at ngrok.com.
  2. On the left dashboard menu, click Your Authtoken.
  3. Copy the token and paste it into the form on the left.
Step 2: Make it Disconnection-Proof (Static Domain)
  1. In your Ngrok dashboard, click Domains in the left menu (under Endpoints) or visit dashboard.ngrok.com/domains.
  2. Click the blue New Domain or Create Domain button.
  3. Ngrok will instantly generate a permanent domain for you (e.g., lion-pleasing.ngrok-free.app).
  4. Copy that exact domain and paste it into the Static Domain field on the left.
  5. Why? If your Wi-Fi disconnects and reconnects, students won't be kicked out because your URL remains identical!
